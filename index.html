About
me

I am a 20 year old artist based in Bristol. I have always had a passion for art and I love to create dream like and whimsical imagery. For most of my work, I use oil paint, put love to explore different mediums. I am so inspired by nature, femininity, and nostalgia, and I think this is reflected in my work.

I post a lot about my work and the behind the scenes on my social media, if you want to follow along!
